Built to be checked.

Payve moves money and writes to systems of record, so it is designed for scrutiny: every automation runs behind a human approval gate, every action is logged, and every customer's data is isolated.

Schedule time with us

Human approval gates

Agents prepare work; people approve it. Nothing posts to your books, your ERP, or your bank without a person on your team clicking approve.

Complete audit trail

Every read, match, write, and payment is logged and traceable to the person who approved it and the record it touched.

Per-customer isolation

Each customer's data lives in its own isolated environment with its own scoped credentials. A request from one customer's agent cannot reach another customer's data.

Credentials vaulted

ERP and system credentials are vaulted and encrypted, never exposed to agents or staff in plain text.

Least-privilege access

API keys carry narrow scopes. A key that can read invoices cannot move money; payment authority is granted separately and explicitly.

Idempotent money movement

Every payment instruction carries a unique key, so a retried request can never move money twice.

About your funds

Payve is a financial technology company, not a bank. Funds held with Payve are safeguarded and always visible: your balance, pending deposits, and scheduled payments each show separately, and every movement is traceable to the invoice or payment behind it.
