## The 20-item checklist (ordered by conversion impact)

1. One primary CTA in the hero, visually dominant (competing CTAs depress conversion; top-SaaS average is 1.4 hero CTAs).
2. Secondary hero CTA only if genuinely different intent (short video / product tour), never a second equal ask.
3. No sign-in in the hero; nav-only ghost.
4. Headline passes the 5-second test: what + for whom, 6-10 words, benefit-led (+31% vs feature headlines).
5. Subhead = who + how in 1-2 sentences, nothing more.
6. Primary CTA above the fold; repeated at 2-3 scroll points + a footer CTA block.
7. Named-logo social proof at/under the fold, anchored by ONE hard number (real platform data only).
8. CTA copy specific and consistent site-wide.
9. Any demo form ≤4 fields; prefer instant scheduler.
10. Show the product above the fold (screenshots, live UI, working-state chips).
11. Outcome numbers in copy, not "AI-powered" adjectives; mechanism lives in subheads.
12. LCP ≤2.5s: preload the homepage hero image; keep heroes ≤~800KB.
13. Sticky nav keeps the demo CTA visible on scroll.
14. No hero carousels/rotators.
15. Solutions pages open pain-first in the vertical's own vocabulary, then outcome, then mechanism, then vertical-specific proof.
16. Proof distributed: legitimacy early, claim-validation mid-page, hesitation-reducers next to each CTA.
17. Mobile hero: headline + CTA visible without scrolling; full-width tap targets.
18. No exit leaks near CTAs (no outbound links or ambiguous "Learn more" in the buyer path).
19. One persona per page.
20. Every number traceable to real data (lexicon rule; no invented metrics ever).
